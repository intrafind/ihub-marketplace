# Prospect Outreach Composer: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Cold outreach to a newly appointed head of quality

**Request**

> I'm Tom Ackermann, AE at Qualis Software (supplier quality management). Prospect: Dr. Katrin Seidel, new Head of
> Quality at Halvorsen Pumpen GmbH, 650 employees. Her LinkedIn says she started in October 2026; she posted that
> her focus is "fewer supplier escapes into production". Halvorsen announced a second plant in Poland on 2 Oct
> (press release). No record in our CRM. Proof point: Reiter Hydraulik cut supplier complaint handling from 12 to 5
> days with us (public case study, attached). Three touches, email plus LinkedIn.

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 1: nothing in the CRM, so net-new outreach; no opt-out to respect.
- Step 4: the strongest hook is rank 1 (new role plus trigger), with her stated priority (rank 2) in the bridge; the
  Poland plant (rank 4) supports touch 3.
- Cadence: three touches with a new angle each: hook plus value, a LinkedIn connection request, then a resource
  offer; no "just following up".
- Every claim about the prospect is sourced; nothing about mutual connections is added.

**Answer**

# Outreach to Dr. Katrin Seidel, Halvorsen Pumpen GmbH

**Channel**: Multi-channel (email and LinkedIn)
**Sequence Length**: 3-touch sequence
**Prospect Context**: Head of Quality since October 2026 at a 650-person pump manufacturer that is adding a
second plant in Poland; publicly focused on fewer supplier escapes into production.

## What the research turned up
- **Role**: Head of Quality, started October 2026 [From LinkedIn]
- **Signals**: Post naming "fewer supplier escapes into production" as her focus [From LinkedIn]; second plant in
  Poland announced 2 Oct 2026 [From user input: press release]; no CRM history [From CRM]
- **Hooks Selected**: 1. New role plus stated priority; 2. New plant and its supplier base

## Touch 1: Opening message
**Subject Line Options**:
1. Your new role and supplier escapes
2. How is Halvorsen tackling supplier escapes?
3. Supplier complaints closed in 5 days, not 12

**Body**:
Dear Dr. Seidel,

congratulations on your new role as Head of Quality at Halvorsen. You wrote that your focus is fewer supplier
escapes into production, and the first months in a role are often when teams take a fresh look at how supplier
issues are caught and closed.

We help quality teams in manufacturing find supplier problems earlier and close complaints faster. Reiter
Hydraulik, for example, cut the time to handle a supplier complaint from 12 to 5 days.

Is reducing supplier escapes something you're planning to work on this quarter?

Best regards,
Tom Ackermann
Qualis Software

---

## Touch 2: LinkedIn connection request
**Timing**: 4 days after Touch 1
**Angle**: Low-commitment second channel, tied to her post

**Subject**: n/a (connection request)

**Body**:
Dr. Seidel, your post on cutting supplier escapes struck a chord: that's what we work on with quality teams in
manufacturing. I'd be glad to connect and follow your work at Halvorsen.

---

## Touch 3: Resource offer
**Timing**: 8 days after Touch 1
**Angle**: New plant: what changes for supplier quality when production expands abroad

**Subject**: Re: Your new role and supplier escapes

**Body**:
Dear Dr. Seidel,

with the second plant in Poland, Halvorsen will likely add new local suppliers, and with them new incoming
inspections and complaint routines to set up.

I've put together a short checklist on bringing new suppliers into an existing quality process, based on what
manufacturing customers like Reiter Hydraulik went through. Should I send it to you?

Best regards,
Tom Ackermann

## Personalization Notes
- Her own words ("supplier escapes") lead touch 1, because a stated priority is more specific than the role change.
- Fallback angle if there's no reply: ask who leads supplier quality for the Poland plant (multi-threading).
- No mutual connections found; don't mention any. The checklist in touch 3 has to exist before sending. [AI draft]

## Example 2: A request to contact someone who opted out (German request)

**Request**

> Schreib mir bitte eine LinkedIn-Nachricht an Markus Engel, IT-Leiter bei Kessler Logistik. Im CRM steht zwar
> „Unsubscribed: ja (03.06.2026)", aber das gilt ja nur für E-Mail, auf LinkedIn kann ich ihn trotzdem anschreiben.

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 1 and ground rule 3: the CRM shows an unsubscribed status, so the skill stops and declines; switching the
  channel does not lift an opt-out.
- It answers in German, explains the restriction briefly and offers what it can do without contacting him.

**Answer**

Diese Nachricht schreibe ich nicht. Im CRM ist Markus Engel seit dem 03.06.2026 als „Unsubscribed" markiert
[From CRM]. Ein Abmeldewunsch richtet sich an unser Unternehmen, nicht an einen Kanal: Wenn wir ihn über LinkedIn
erneut ansprechen, umgehen wir seine Entscheidung, und das kann je nach Einwilligungslage auch datenschutzrechtlich
heikel sein.

Was ich stattdessen tun kann:
- **Andere Ansprechpartner bei Kessler Logistik:** Wenn Sie mir Namen aus dem CRM oder von LinkedIn nennen, prüfe
  ich deren Status und entwerfe eine Ansprache, sofern kein Opt-out vorliegt.
- **Klärung:** Wenn Sie glauben, dass der Eintrag falsch ist (etwa ein Fehler beim Import), klären Sie das bitte
  mit Marketing oder dem Datenschutz, bevor wir ihn kontaktieren.
- **Wenn er sich selbst meldet:** Fragt Herr Engel von sich aus an, etwa über ein Webformular, helfe ich gern bei
  der Antwort.
