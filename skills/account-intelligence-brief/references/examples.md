# Account Intelligence Brief: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: First-meeting prep for a net-new account

**Request**

> I have a first call with Kestrel Fördertechnik GmbH on 24 November. We sell a quality-management SaaS for
> manufacturers. No CRM connected here, so here is what I have:
> - Kestrel: conveyor systems, HQ Augsburg, ~850 employees (LinkedIn), sells to logistics centres and airports.
> - 2 Nov 2026 press release: new COO Petra Lindqvist, ex-plant director at one of our customers, Norrvik AB.
> - 21 Oct 2026, trade press: Kestrel opens a second plant in Brno, production start Q2 2027.
> - Job ad from 5 Nov 2026: "Head of Quality Systems", mentions ISO 9001 recertification and "replacing
>   spreadsheet-based CAPA tracking".
> - Our CRM (I checked myself): one demo in 2023 with their quality manager Jonas Brenner, no deal.

**How the skill handles it**

- Situation table: "Prepping a first meeting", so the complete brief, with most room for hooks and people.
- Stage 1: no CRM connection, so the CRM section rests on the user's own check, tagged (per user).
- Ground rule 2: no revenue was given, so it reads "No public figure available".
- Stage 4: Lindqvist comes from a customer (lateral warm path); the job ad's hiring manager is a likely champion,
  listed as "Still to be identified".
- All signals are under 30 days old; the two hooks are ranked by how directly they hit the CAPA pain.

## Example 2: Territory scan with thin information

**Request**

> Quick territory scan, which of these should I work first? Ormbach Medical (Freiburg),
> Velde Logistics (Rotterdam), Sandor Plastics (Győr). I only know: Ormbach got a Series C of EUR 40m in
> September 2026 (their press release). Velde: no idea. Sandor: our CRM shows a lost deal in May 2024
> ("lost to incumbent, price"). Our ICP is manufacturers with 300-2,000 employees.

**How the skill handles it**

- Situation table: "Planning a territory", so a light scan per account, not three full briefs.
- Ground rule 2: headcount has no source anywhere, so it reads "No public figure available" and ICP fit stays
  unconfirmed.
- "Whenever a source is missing, say so": nothing is known about Velde, and the scan says so instead of guessing.
- Signal weighting: the Sandor loss is older than 90 days, so it is flagged as stale.
