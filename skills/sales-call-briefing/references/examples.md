# Sales Call Briefing: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Tailored demo with a known champion

**Request**

> Prep the Brightwater Utilities demo, Thu 20 Nov, 14:00–15:00. CRM: Evaluation, €96,000/yr, 34 days in stage,
> close 31 Jan. Discovery 4 Nov with champion Sanne Holm (Head of Field Service): 2-day lag between field work
> orders and billing; wants mobile work orders and SAP sync. Also invited: Piet de Wit (IT architect, raised SSO
> and data residency by email 6 Nov; statement sent 10 Nov) and Lotte Bakker (field team lead). Goal: a 4-week pilot.

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 4: the user named the primary objective; the others are derived and marked as AI suggestions.
- Step 5: demo flow, features ordered by each attendee's stated priorities.
- Step 2: Lotte Bakker has no CRM history, so the brief plans discovery questions for her.

**Answer**

## Example 2: Negotiation call with an unknown attendee (German)

**Request**

> Morgen 10 Uhr, 45 Min., Verhandlung mit Kessler Druckguss GmbH. CRM: Negotiation, 148.000 €, Close 15.12.
> 12.11.: Angebot an Einkäufer Martin Roth (Economic Buyer); er will 12 % Nachlass und 60 Tage Zahlungsziel.
> Neu auf der Einladung: „F. Albers“, nicht im CRM. Was ist mein Ziel?

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 4: no objectives given, so all are derived from stage and attendees, marked as AI suggestions.
- Ground rule 1: "F. Albers" gets the fixed marker; no role is guessed.
- Negotiation flow; no discount figure is suggested, only how to handle the request.

**Answer**
