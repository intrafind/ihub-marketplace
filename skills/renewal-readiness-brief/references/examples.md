# Renewal Readiness Brief: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Healthy account with an expansion signal

**Request**

> Prep the Kestrel Logistics renewal. ARR €84,000, 250 seats, renews 31 Jan 2027, paid on time. Health check last
> week: Product 5, Support 4, Relationship 4, Commercial 4, Sentiment 4, Outcomes 5. 212 of 250 seats active (170 in
> May). Warehouse team (~40 people) asked for access in the October QBR. QBR ROI: planning time -31%, 2 FTE
> redeployed. Champion Mira Hollandt (Head of Ops); CFO attended the QBR. One escalation in August, fixed in 3 days,
> CSAT 4.6. No reorg news. I'm Lena Brandt, AE Tobias Kern.

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 1: the health scores from last week are recent enough; no new assessment.
- Step 2: every category rates Low, so overall risk is Low (worst single category).
- Step 3: one signal with evidence: the warehouse team's request (seat expansion).
- Step 4: Low risk plus a signal gives **Expand**. The estimate uses the contract's own rate (€84,000 / 250 =
  €336 per seat); no new price point is proposed.

**Answer**

## Example 2: Lost champion and a discount demand (German)

**Request**

> Renewal-Brief für die Altmann Präzisionsteile AG: ARR 126.000 €, Verlängerung 31.03.2027. Health-Check von
> gestern: Product 2, Support 3, Relationship 1, Commercial 3, Sentiment 2, Outcomes 2. Aktive Nutzer 180 → 120
> (200 Lizenzen). Champion Dr. Petra Vogt im September gegangen, Nachfolge unbekannt. Einkauf will 15 % Rabatt,
> „schaut sich Alternativen an“. Zwei Tickets offen, eins eskaliert. Welchen Rabatt bieten wir an? Ich bin Jan Weber.

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 2: Usage and Relationship are High, so the overall risk is High; step 4 gives **Recover**.
- Ground rules: no discount figure; the brief lays out the approach and hands the numbers to pricing.
- Ground rules: budget and competitor are unknown, so it asks instead of assuming.
- Step 3: no signal qualifies, so the template's no-expansion sentence is used.

## Gesprächsplan
- Einstieg: „Die letzten Monate liefen nicht, wie Sie es erwartet haben. Klären wir zuerst, was sich ändern muss.“
