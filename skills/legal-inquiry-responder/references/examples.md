# Legal Inquiry Responder: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Litigation hold after a statement of claim

**Request**

> We (Ostwind Automation GmbH, Germany) received a statement of claim yesterday, 16 Nov 2026, from our former
> distributor Talvik Trading AB, over termination of the distribution agreement and unpaid commissions. Regional
> Court Munich I. Please draft the hold notice. Relevant people: Sales Director Benedikt Arnold, account managers
> Lea Brunner, Kai Seidel and Noemi Frick, contracts manager Rolf Ebner. Data: email, Teams chats, SharePoint site
> "Distribution Partners", CRM account records for Talvik. Relationship started 1 Jan 2023. Questions go to
> in-house counsel Dr. Sabine Marx. Our reference LH-2026-007.

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 1: pending litigation, so **Litigation hold notice**; jurisdiction (Germany, Munich court)
  is stated, so nothing to ask.
- Fills every required component and the scope sheet from the facts given; search terms are offered to help
  locate material, never to narrow the hold.
- Hold lifecycle: issuance within 24–48 hours of the claim (17 Nov), acknowledgment within 5 business days
  (24 Nov 2026), first reminder inside 60–90 days.
- Counsel review is marked as required; no escalation trigger applies yet.

**Answer**

> Not legal advice. This draft requires review by a qualified legal professional before it is sent.

**Preservation scope sheet**

| Dimension | Entry |
|---|---|
| Matter | Talvik Trading AB v. Ostwind Automation GmbH, Regional Court Munich I; ref. LH-2026-007 |
| Period covered | From 1 Jan 2023 until ongoing |
| Custodians | Benedikt Arnold, Lea Brunner, Kai Seidel, Noemi Frick, Rolf Ebner |
| Kinds of data | Email, chat messages, files, CRM records |
| Systems | Mailboxes, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint site "Distribution Partners", CRM account "Talvik" |
| Search terms | Talvik, distribution agreement, commission, termination (to locate material only) |
| Carve-outs | None |

**Hold notice**

Subject: Legal hold — preserve all material on Talvik Trading AB (ref. LH-2026-007)

To: Benedikt Arnold, Lea Brunner, Kai Seidel, Noemi Frick, Rolf Ebner
Date: 17 November 2026

On 16 November 2026 Talvik Trading AB filed a claim against Ostwind Automation GmbH at the Regional Court Munich I
concerning the termination of the distribution agreement and commission payments. You hold information that may
be relevant.

**What you must keep.** From today, preserve all documents and data relating to Talvik Trading AB, the
distribution agreement, its termination and commissions, created from 1 January 2023 onwards. This includes
emails, Teams chats, files on the SharePoint site "Distribution Partners", CRM records, notes and drafts, in any
format and wherever stored.

**Routine deletion stops.** Do not use automatic deletion, mailbox clean-up or archive purges for this material.
IT will suspend retention deletion for these accounts and systems.

**What you must not do.** Do not delete, alter or overwrite any of this material, and do not move it to personal
devices or private accounts.

**How long.** This hold applies until the Legal department releases it in writing.

**If it is not followed.** Losing or changing relevant material can damage the company's position in court and
lead to sanctions.

**Questions or new material.** Contact Dr. Sabine Marx, Legal, about the scope, or if you find further material or
know of material that may already have been deleted.

**Please confirm by 24 November 2026** that you have received and understood this notice by replying "Confirmed"
to this email.

Legal Department, Ostwind Automation GmbH

**Hold management**

| Stage | Action | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Issuance | Send notice; ask IT to suspend deletion for the listed accounts and systems | 17 Nov 2026 |
| Acknowledgment | Collect all five confirmations | by 24 Nov 2026 |
| Reminder | First reminder to all custodians | 26 Jan 2027 |
| Scope update | Supplemental notice if new custodians or systems emerge | as needed |
| Release | Only on written authorization by Legal | — |

Escalate to litigation counsel at once if a custodian reports deleted material or refuses to confirm, or if the
hold must reach external providers.

## Example 2: Regulator letter naming employees, short deadline

**Request**

> Letter arrived today (Tue 17 Nov 2026) from the State Data Protection Authority, ref. LfD-2026-4412, contact
> Ms. Orlova. Following a complaint, they ask how our HR department processed health data of two named employees
> during their sick leave, and want our answer and the relevant records by Mon 23 Nov 2026. Can you draft the
> answer? We're in Germany.

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 1: **Regulatory inquiry**, kind *Targeted* (arises from a complaint, aimed at the organization).
- Step 3 triggers apply: specific individuals are named, and only 4 business days remain (18–23 Nov), under 5,
  so the General Counsel or external counsel takes over immediately.
- Regulatory playbook: log it at once and acknowledge receipt, nothing more; no substantive answer is drafted.
- Health data (special category) is flagged for counsel.

**Answer**

> Not legal advice. This draft requires review by a qualified legal professional before it is sent.

I won't draft the substantive answer: this inquiry has to go to the General Counsel or external counsel
immediately. Two escalation triggers apply. The letter names specific employees, and fewer than 5 business days
remain before the deadline (18, 19, 20 and 23 November). It also concerns health data, a special category, and
followed a complaint, which makes it a targeted inquiry where counsel leads and the facts are established
internally first.

**1. Log entry (do now)**

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Received | 17 Nov 2026 |
| Authority | State Data Protection Authority |
| Contact | Ms. Orlova |
| Reference | LfD-2026-4412 |
| Subject | HR processing of health data of two named employees during sick leave; follows a complaint |
| Deadline | 23 Nov 2026 |
| Kind | Targeted |
| Escalated to | [General Counsel / external counsel], on 17 Nov 2026 |

**2. Acknowledgment of receipt (for counsel to approve; no substance)**

Subject: Your letter of 17 November 2026, ref. LfD-2026-4412

Dear Ms. Orlova,

We confirm receipt of your letter of 17 November 2026, reference LfD-2026-4412. We are reviewing your request and
will respond within the time set. Please direct further correspondence on this matter to [name, function, contact
details].

Kind regards,
[Name, function]

**3. For counsel**
- Decide whether to ask for an extension of the 23 November deadline.
- Make sure the HR records concerned are preserved and not changed while the inquiry is open.
- Send no records or explanations before counsel has reviewed them.
