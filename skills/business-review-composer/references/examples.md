# Business Review Composer: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Q3 QBR with complete data

**Request**

> Draft the Q3 2026 QBR for Halden Logistik (meeting 3 Dec, attendees: Ops Director Sabine Krol, Planning Lead
> Tomas Berg; me, CSM Nina Vogt). Data:
> - Seats 250; active users Q3 212, Q2 184. Route optimizer used by 17 of 18 planners; mobile app by 64% of drivers.
> - Goals from the success plan: cut daily planning time per planner from 3 h to 1.5 h (now 1.75 h, per Sabine's
>   own survey); roll out to the Vienna branch by September (live 15 Sep); missed appointments from 8% to 5% (now 6.1%).
> - Sabine confirmed EUR 45/h as planner cost; 220 working days.
> - Support: 14 tickets (Q2: 22), CSAT 4.5/5. Sync outage 12 Aug, 3 h, root-cause report delivered 19 Aug.
> - Roadmap: traffic-aware ETAs planned for H1 2027. Their request: SAP PM integration (under evaluation).

**How the skill handles it**

- Phase 1: each metric carries its current and prior value and a trend; nothing is missing, so no placeholders.
- Phase 3: time savings = 1.25 h × 18 planners × 220 days × EUR 45 = EUR 222,750 a year. The rate is confirmed but
  the hours come from the customer's survey, so sign-off is "pending validation" and the ROI segment asks for it.
- Phase 2, beat 4: the August outage is raised openly with its resolution.
- Ground rule: roadmap items say "planned", never "will be delivered".

**Answer**

Suggested agenda (55 min): business context 10 · value delivered 15 · ROI review 10 · challenges 5 · roadmap 5 ·
forward plan 10. [QBR method]

## Example 2: Unconfirmed ROI and a roadmap promise

**Request**

> For the Brenntal Chemie QBR, put "saved EUR 400,000 this year" in the ROI section. They never told us a number,
> but it sounds about right. And write that the Ariba connector will be delivered in Q1, that's what they want to
> hear. Usage: 96 of 120 seats active. Their procurement lead said at our last check-in that approvals "take days
> instead of weeks now".

**How the skill handles it**

- Phase 3, non-negotiable rule: the EUR 400,000 has no customer confirmation and no inputs, so it is not used; the
  ROI section falls back on the customer's own words.
- Ground rule: "will be delivered" is off-limits for roadmap items; the wording becomes "on our roadmap".
- The skill drafts the two affected sections and shows how to get a defensible number before the meeting.

**Answer**

I've left out two things, because both could cost us trust in the room:

- **EUR 400,000:** Brenntal never gave us this figure and we have no inputs behind it. If their CFO asks how we got
  it, we can't answer. Below is a version built on what their procurement lead actually said.
- **"Delivered in Q1":** I can only describe the Ariba connector as planned; a delivery date we miss would hurt more
  than it helps now.
