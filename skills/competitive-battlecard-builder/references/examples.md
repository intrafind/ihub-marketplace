# Competitive Battlecard Builder: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Full battlecard for a high-complexity RFP

**Request**

> Battlecard against DocuStream for the Stadtwerke Hessenau RFP (document management, 1,200 users, 9-month cycle,
> six people on the evaluation board). RFP must-haves: on-premises operation (they are critical infrastructure) and
> SAP integration. Nice-to-haves: Microsoft 365 integration, modern UI. Our intel: DocuStream is cloud-only (their
> website, checked last week); implementation only through partners; list price per user per month plus storage
> fees above 1 TB (their price page). From our win/loss notes: we lost Kreiswerke Altenbach to them in 2026 on UI and
> Teams integration. Our proof point: Stadtwerke Nordau runs us on-premises with SAP since 2023.

**How the skill handles it**

- Complexity: long cycle, six stakeholders, critical infrastructure. High, so the complete card with landmine questions.
- Step 3: the matrix uses only the RFP's criteria; on-premises is primary differentiation, UI and M365 are
  DocuStream's ground to neutralize.
- Every claim carries its source label; the pricing section compares models, never quotes prices.
- Step 7: head-to-head scenario, so the card leads with the must-win criteria.

**Answer**

**Battlecard: DocuStream, Stadtwerke Hessenau**
*Verify competitive claims before customer-facing use.*

**1. Competitor overview**
Cloud-only document management sold and implemented through partners [From user input]. Won Kreiswerke Altenbach
against us on user interface and Teams integration [From battlecard/knowledge source].

**Positioning matrix** (scenario: head-to-head)

| Criterion | Type | Us | DocuStream | Angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| On-premises operation | Must-win | Strong | Weak (cloud-only) | Primary differentiation |
| SAP integration | Must-win | Strong (Nordau reference) | Neutral (no evidence either way) | Prove with reference |
| Microsoft 365 integration | Nice-to-have | Neutral | Strong (Altenbach loss) | Neutralize |
| Modern UI | Nice-to-have | Neutral | Strong (Altenbach loss) | Neutralize |

[AI framework application]

**2. Their strengths and how to neutralize them**
- **Fact:** DocuStream won Altenbach on UI and Teams integration [From battlecard/knowledge source].
  **Impact:** Board members who use the system daily may favour it in demos, but both points are nice-to-haves in this RFP.
  **Act:** Ask the board to weight criteria before demos; show our Teams add-in on a Hessenau use case.

**3. Their weaknesses and how to exploit them**
- **Fact:** DocuStream is cloud-only [From user input: DocuStream website].
  **Impact:** Hessenau requires on-premises operation as critical infrastructure; a cloud-only product misses a must-have.
  **Act:** Ask: "How will your KRITIS auditors view documents held outside your own data centre?" Then share Nordau.
- **Fact:** Implementation runs only through partners [From user input].
  **Impact:** A six-person board with a 9-month cycle will want one accountable party.
  **Act:** Offer a named project lead from our side in the proposal.

**4. Pricing comparison framework**
| Factor | Us | DocuStream |
|---|---|---|
| Model type | Your model | Per user per month [From user input: price page] |
| Storage | Your terms | Fees above 1 TB [From user input: price page] |
| Implementation | Your services | Partner services, priced separately |
| Value per unit | Total 5-year cost ÷ 1,200 users | Same formula |

**5. Objection-response pairs (LAER)**
*Capability gaps: "DocuStream's interface is more modern."*
- Listen: let the evaluator finish without defending.
- Acknowledge: "Ease of use matters for 1,200 people."
- Explore: "Which tasks would they do most often? Who raised this?"
- Respond: demo those tasks; point to Nordau's user adoption.

*Pricing, Compliance/Security, References, Vendor maturity:* Competitive data needed.

**6. Landmine questions**
- Dependency/switching: "If you had to change course later, how portable would your documents be?"
- Total cost of ownership: "Apart from the quoted price, which costs go into your evaluation?" (storage, partner services)
- Implementation complexity: "What does a rollout usually look like for you, and what resources do you set aside?"

**7. Customer proof points**
| Customer | Situation | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stadtwerke Nordau | On-premises with SAP | Live since 2023 | [From user input] |

## Example 2: Quick request with no competitive data

**Request**

> Short call with Fenner Logistik in an hour, a 20-seat renewal. They said Brightline is cheaper. What do I say
> about Brightline?

**How the skill handles it**

- Complexity is low (small renewal, short call), so the output is a focused FIA summary, not a full card.
- No intel about Brightline was given, so the competitor-specific parts read "Competitive data needed"; the skill
  does not fill them from memory.
- What it can deliver without claims: the LAER structure for the price objection and TCO landmine questions.

**Answer**

*Verify competitive claims before customer-facing use.*

**What I can't give you:** anything about Brightline itself. Competitive data needed: their pricing model, what is
included, and any known gaps. If your team has a battlecard library or a recent win/loss note, share it, or I can
run a web search on their published pricing.

**For the call: handle "Brightline is cheaper" with LAER** [AI framework application]
- **Listen:** Let them say the number and what it covers.
- **Acknowledge:** "Budget matters, especially on a renewal."
- **Explore:** "Is that a like-for-like quote? What is included in it?" and "Is price the main reason you'd consider
  switching, or is something else not working?" A price objection in a renewal often stands in for an unsolved problem.
- **Respond:** Only once you know the root concern. If it really is price, compare total cost, not list price.

**Landmine questions (pick two)**
- Total cost of ownership: "Apart from the quoted price, which other costs go into your evaluation?"
- Dependency/switching: "If you switched, how would your data and current setup move over?"

**FIA point to fill after the call**
- Fact: Competitive data needed.
- Impact: what a switch would mean for Fenner's 20 users and their workflows.
- Act: the follow-up you agree on.
