# Email Campaign Creator: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Webinar registration for existing customers

**Request**

> Campaign for our webinar "Predictive maintenance in 45 minutes" on 3 December, 10:00 CET, 300 seats. Audience:
> active customers of our maintenance software Planwerk (about 1,400 companies). Goal: registrations. No discount.
> Voice: professional, friendly. Short emails. Speaker: Dr. Mira Hold, our head of data science.

**How the skill handles it**

- Information gathering: goal, audience, product, offer (none), voice and length are all given, so no questions.
- Active customers: per the segmentation table, a grateful insider tone and exclusive access as the angle.
- Variant A leads with the benefit, Variant B with a short scenario; one primary CTA each; the 300 seats and the
  1,400 customers are the only scarcity and proof used, because the user supplied them.

**Answer**

### Campaign Brief
- **Campaign name**: WEB-2026-12 Predictive Maintenance, customers
- **Send date recommendation**: Tuesday 24 November, 09:00 CET, which leaves 9 days to register; a reminder to
  non-openers on 1 December
- **List segment**: active Planwerk customers, maintenance and operations contacts
- **Success metrics**: open rate, CTR, registrations (target: fill the 300 seats)

---

**Subject Line A**: Predict failures before they stop your line
**Preview Text A**: 45-minute live session for Planwerk customers on 3 December. 300 seats.

### Subject Line Options
1. **Curiosity**: What your machines told you last month
2. **Benefit**: Plan repairs before machines fail
3. **Urgency**: 300 seats: predictive maintenance live
4. **Personal**: {{first_name}}, your data can predict failures
5. **Question**: Which machine will fail next?

### CTA Variations
- Primary: Reserve my seat →
- Secondary: Send me the recording
- Urgency: Grab one of 300 seats →

### Segmentation Recommendations
| Segment | Tone Adjustment | CTA Focus | Subject Line Angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active customers (this send) | Grateful, insider | Register | Exclusive access |
| Prospects (if extended later) | Professional, credibility | Register, then demo | Problem-solution |

### Post-Campaign Analysis
```
EXPECTED BENCHMARKS (Industry averages):
- Open Rate: 20-25%
- Click-Through Rate: 2-5%
- Conversion Rate: 1-3%
- Unsubscribe Rate: <0.5%

A/B TEST SUCCESS CRITERIA:
- Minimum sample size per variant: 1,000 recipients
- Test duration: 4 hours before selecting winner
- Winner threshold: 95% statistical confidence
- Winning metric: CTR to the registration page
```

Technical: send a plain-text version alongside the HTML, 600px single column, alt text on the hero image, 16px+
body text. The footer needs your physical address, a working unsubscribe link and a clear sender name (GDPR).

## Example 2: Request too thin to write

**Request**

> Write me an email campaign for our new product. Make it pop.

**How the skill handles it**

- Information gathering: goal, audience, product, offer and length are all missing; copy written now would rest on
  guesses about every one of them, so the skill asks first.
- It bundles the questions into one short list with examples, so a single reply is enough.
- "Make it pop" is a voice hint; it confirms it rather than ignoring it.

**Answer**

Glad to. To make the campaign land, I need a few basics; short notes are enough:

1. **Goal:** what should the reader do? Buy, book a demo, register, read a page?
2. **Audience:** existing customers, prospects, new subscribers or lapsed customers? Roughly how many recipients?
3. **Product:** name, what it solves and for whom, in one or two sentences.
4. **Offer:** is there a launch discount, a deadline or a limited quantity?
5. **Voice:** I read "make it pop" as bold and direct. Does that fit your brand, or rather factual with a strong
   message?
6. **Length:** short (under 200 words) or more detailed?

Once I have that, you get the full package: campaign brief, two A/B variants with subject line and preview text,
five subject line options, three CTA variations, notes per segment and the measurement framework.
