## Example 2: Thin brief with a budget cap (German)

**Request**

> Schreib ein Angebot für die Stadtwerke Lindenau: Kantinen-App zum Vorbestellen von Essen. Budget max. 25.000 EUR.
> Mehr weiß ich noch nicht. Wir sind die Appwerk GmbH.

**How the skill handles it**

- Information gathering: scope, timeline and readers are missing; reasonable assumptions are stated at the top.
- The budget cap shapes scope: a lean web app; native apps are optional.
- Pricing: 4,400 + 14,000 + 3,000 = 21,400 net; with 19% VAT (4,066) the gross 25,466 exceeds the cap, so "net"
  is flagged as an assumption.
