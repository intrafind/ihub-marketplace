# SEO Content Optimizer: worked examples

Two complete examples of this skill's work. The names, companies and figures are invented: keep the structure and
the way of working, never reuse the facts.

## Example 1: Blog draft without structure

**Request**

> Optimize this blog draft (Maintora, maintenance software; readers: plant and maintenance managers): "Downtime is a big problem. When a machine stops it costs money and our customers have told us that they lose a lot of production because maintenance was done too late or it was done when it was not needed. There is a better way that is used by more and more companies and it is based on data. Sensors are installed on the machines and the data is collected and then it is analysed so that problems can be detected before they happen. This is called predictive maintenance. Our software Maintora helps with this. Contact us for a demo."

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 1: informational intent; 100 words, no headings, keyword once (1.0%).
- Step 3: keyword first in title (55 characters) and H1; meta description 154 characters.
- Step 4: question headings and a numbered list suit featured snippets and FAQ/HowTo schema.
- Rewrite: "predictive maintenance" 3 times in 187 words (1.6%).

**Answer**

## SEO Analysis Report

### Current State
One 100-word paragraph: no headings, no answer to "how", a 35-word sentence, mostly passive voice ("is
installed", "is collected"). The primary keyword appears once, near the end. Flesch Reading Ease is about 70 thanks
to short words, but the sentences are hard to follow.

### Keyword Strategy
- Primary: predictive maintenance - Target density: 1.5%
- Primary (supporting): machine downtime, unplanned downtime
- Secondary: condition monitoring, vibration analysis, sensor data, maintenance planning, failure prediction

### Optimized Title & Meta
- Title: Predictive Maintenance: Cut Machine Downtime in 5 Steps
- Meta: Learn how predictive maintenance cuts unplanned machine downtime in 5 practical steps for mid-size plants, from sensor data to alerts. Read the guide now.
- H1: Predictive Maintenance: How to Cut Unplanned Machine Downtime
- URL: /predictive-maintenance-reduce-downtime

### Content Score
- Readability: 50/100
- Keyword optimization: 35/100
- Structure: 15/100
- Overall SEO Score: 33/100

### Priority Improvements
1. Add an H1 and question-style H2s; mark them up with FAQ schema.
2. Answer "how does it work" directly under its heading (featured-snippet format).
3. Split the 35-word sentence; use active voice.
4. Add a numbered 5-step list (HowTo schema).
5. Link to your product page and a case study; give any images alt text ("vibration sensor on a CNC spindle").

### Optimized Content
[H1] Predictive Maintenance: How to Cut Unplanned Machine Downtime

Every hour a production line stands still costs output, overtime and delivery dates. Fixed maintenance intervals don't solve this: parts get replaced too early, or a failure hits before the next service. Predictive maintenance takes a different route. It uses machine data to spot wear before it turns into unplanned downtime.

[H2] How does predictive maintenance work?
It collects sensor data such as vibration, temperature and power draw and compares it with each machine's normal pattern. When the pattern drifts, it raises an alert. Maintenance then happens when the machine needs it, not when the calendar says so.

[H2] 5 steps to start with predictive maintenance
1. Pick the machines whose failures cost the most.
2. Fit sensors for vibration and temperature.
3. Record a baseline of normal operation.
4. Set alert thresholds with your maintenance team.
5. Review alerts weekly and adjust the thresholds.

[H2] What changes for your maintenance team?
Instead of fixed rounds, your team plans work around alerts. Spare parts can be ordered ahead, and repairs move into planned stops.

Maintora helps you set this up on your own machines. Book a demo to see how.

## Example 2: Keyword-stuffed product page, request for 5% density (German)

**Request**

> Unsere Produktseite rankt nicht. Bitte optimieren und das Keyword „Hydraulikschlauch“ auf mindestens 5 % Dichte
> bringen. Text: „Hydraulikschlauch kaufen beim Hydraulikschlauch-Profi! Unsere Hydraulikschläuche sind die besten Hydraulikschläuche. Hydraulikschlauch nach Maß, Hydraulikschlauch günstig, Hydraulikschlauch schnell. Wir fertigen jeden Hydraulikschlauch in 24 Stunden. Hydraulikschlauch für Bagger, Hydraulikschlauch für Traktor. Hydraulikschlauch bis 400 bar. Jetzt Hydraulikschlauch bestellen!“

**How the skill handles it**

- Step 1: transactional intent; the keyword is already 12 of 37 words (32%).
- Closing rule: intent beats keyword stuffing, so it declines the 5% target; the rewrite lands at 1.9% (2 of 106
  words), using only facts from the original.
- Step 3: title 52, meta 156 characters; the answer is in German.

**Answer**

## SEO-Analysebericht

### Ist-Zustand
37 Wörter, davon 12 Mal „Hydraulikschlauch“ oder „Hydraulikschläuche“ (32 %): Keyword-Stuffing, und Besucher
erfahren nicht, was sie angeben müssen. 5 % empfehle ich nicht, das würde das Ranking eher schwächen. Ziel: 1–2 %.

### Keyword-Strategie
- Primär: Hydraulikschlauch nach Maß - Zieldichte: 1–2 %
- Sekundär: Schlauchleitung, Ersatzschlauch Bagger, Hydraulikschlauch Traktor, Hydraulikschlauch anfertigen lassen

### Optimierter Title & Meta
- Title: Hydraulikschlauch nach Maß – Lieferung in 24 Stunden
- Meta: Hydraulikschlauch nach Maß, gefertigt und geliefert in 24 Stunden. Für Bagger, Traktor und mehr, bis 400 bar. Senden Sie uns Maße oder ein Foto der Leitung.
- H1: Hydraulikschlauch nach Maß in 24 Stunden
- URL: /hydraulikschlauch-nach-mass

### Content-Score
- Lesbarkeit: 40/100
- Keyword-Optimierung: 10/100
- Struktur: 10/100
- Gesamt-SEO-Score: 20/100

### Wichtigste Verbesserungen
1. Keyword-Wiederholungen streichen und durch Varianten ersetzen (Schlauchleitung, Ersatzschlauch).
2. Fragen als H2 aufnehmen, die Käufer vor der Bestellung haben (FAQ-Schema).
3. Konkret sagen, welche Angaben für eine Bestellung nötig sind.
4. Produkt-Schema ergänzen; Alt-Text für Produktfotos, z. B. „Hydraulikschlauch mit Anschlüssen“.

Fällt an Bagger oder Traktor eine Leitung aus, steht die Arbeit. Wir fertigen Ihren Hydraulikschlauch nach Maß und liefern ihn innerhalb von 24 Stunden.
